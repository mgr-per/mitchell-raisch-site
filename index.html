01 / About

Mitchell has spent 15 years building and scaling consumer health and wellness brands. The through-line has been his work from the operator’s chair across the core value-creation levers: channel expansion, unit-economics repair, leadership rebuilds, supply-chain redesign, and board and investor work.

Today, he is Founding Partner of Underhook Partners, advising private equity sponsors and consumer health, wellness, and CPG companies on diligence, growth strategy, and operating plans.

Most recently, he was CEO of Snap Kitchen from October 2023 through August 2026. The vertically integrated prepared-meals company, backed by investors including L Catterton, included 10+ Texas retail stores, distribution in 40+ Whole Foods Market locations, a DTC subscription, and a 30,000-square-foot production kitchen. During his tenure, the business delivered back-to-back years of double-digit growth, improved profitability, and evaluated strategic growth options with the board.

Before that, Mitchell served as Chief Marketing Officer and then Global General Manager of F45 Training from 2017 through May 2023. He helped guide the business through its $325M NYSE IPO as the network grew to 2,100 studios across 60+ countries.

He started as a founder, co-founding The Chill Group and building Just Chill from formulation to shelves at Whole Foods, Kroger, and H-E-B ahead of its 2018 acquisition. That experience continues to shape his operating approach: pairing brand building and demand generation with rigorous unit economics and modern, AI-enabled operating infrastructure.
